Questions

Do I need a separate account per location?

No. One account covers every location on your plan. Each site manager sees only their own inventory; ops sees all of them.

Can I switch plans as I open new locations?

Yes, plans are billed per active location and adjust automatically the month a new site goes live.

Is there a setup fee?

Single Site and Multi-Location have no setup fee. Enterprise includes a paid onboarding package for vendor integrations.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export full inventory and order history as CSV at any time, including after cancellation, for 90 days.
