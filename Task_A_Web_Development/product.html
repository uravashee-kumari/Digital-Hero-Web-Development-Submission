Product

Four systems, one dashboard

All four of these run off the same live count. Nothing gets re-typed between them, which is the part people usually don't believe until they see it.

01

Par-level tracking

Set a par per item, per location. Larder shows current stock against that par in real time, pulled from scans at receiving and prep — not a once-a-week manual count.

02

Automatic reorder drafts

When an item drops below par, Larder drafts the vendor order — quantities, vendor, price from the last invoice — and a manager approves it in one tap instead of building it from scratch.

03

Spoilage logging

Expiring stock is flagged before it's wasted, not after. Every write-off is logged with a reason, so the monthly spoilage number stops being a mystery.

04

Vendor invoice matching

Incoming invoices are checked line-by-line against what was actually delivered and what was ordered, so pricing discrepancies get caught before they're paid.
